## 摘要
机器学习（ML）仿真器为基于集合的资料同化（DA）中生成对流允许背景集合提供了一种经济高效的替代数值天气预报模型的方法。然而，很少有研究探索基于ML的替代背景集合来估计背景误差协方差（BECs）。本研究开发了一个对流允许仿真器MAPCast，该仿真器基于来自跨尺度预报模型（MPAS）的历史对流允许模拟进行训练，并评估其估计BECs的能力，为多尺度DA铺平道路。评估使用了10个回顾性对流个例，预报时效分别为15分钟和60分钟，对应次每小时和每小时DA。MAPCast以良好的保真度重现了MPAS预报，包括真实的风暴覆盖、时间演变以及状态变量的相似空间和谱特征。差异主要局限于陡峭梯度附近的小空间尺度以及对流尺度特征和变量。对于BEC统计量，MAPCast捕获了大多数变量的集合离散度大小和空间分布，尽管垂直速度和反射率等风暴相关场存在较大误差。相关结构在中小尺度及以上尺度上最忠实地再现，其次是对流尺度，而跨变量相关性的表示不如单变量相关性准确，表明多变量耦合仍然是主要限制。MAPCast在全尺度相关性的再现上弱于分解后的大尺度和小尺度相关性。从15分钟预报导出的BEC估计始终优于60分钟预报，表明较短的预报时效能更好地保留流依赖的误差结构。

## Abstract
Machine learning (ML) emulators offer a cost-efficient alternative to numerical weather prediction models for generating convection-allowing background ensembles in ensemble-based data assimilation (DA). However, few studies have explored ML-based surrogate background ensembles for estimating background-error covariances (BECs). This study develops a convection-allowing emulator, MAPCast, trained on historical convection-allowing simulations from the Model for Prediction Across Scales (MPAS), and evaluates its ability to estimate BECs, paving the way toward multiscale DA. The evaluation uses 10 retrospective convective cases at 15- and 60-min forecast lead times corresponding to subhourly and hourly DA. MAPCast reproduces MPAS forecasts with good fidelity, including realistic storm coverage, temporal evolution, and similar spatial and spectral characteristics of state variables. Discrepancies are primarily confined to small spatial scales near sharp gradients and convective-scale features and variables. For BEC statistics, MAPCast captures ensemble spread magnitude and spatial distribution for most variables, although larger errors occur for storm-related fields that are vertical velocity and reflectivity. Correlation structures are reproduced most faithfully at mesoscale and above, followed by at convective scales, whereas cross-variable correlations are less accurately represented than univariate correlations, indicating that multivariate coupling remains the principal limitation. MAPCast shows weaker replication of full-scale versus decomposed large and small-scale correlations. BEC estimates derived from 15-min forecasts consistently outperform those from 60-min forecasts, suggesting that shorter lead times better preserve flow-dependent error structures.
