## 摘要
我们考虑从状态线性函数的观测值重构非线性动力系统状态的问题。我们提出了一种可调观测器的设计方法，并给出了一般定理，该定理在某些条件下保证了观测器无论初始误差如何都能指数收敛。还提供了将该定理应用于化学反应网络模型的附加结果。此外，这些结果通过观察化学物质浓度子集的质量作用形式的化学反应网络示例进行了说明。数值结果展示了所提出的观测器的有效性。还展示了观测噪声情况下的数值结果，并且在观测噪声较小时，我们的观测器优于粒子滤波器。

## Abstract
We consider the problem of state reconstruction for a nonlinear dynamical system from observations of a linear function of the state. We present a design method for a tunable observer and provide a general theorem which under certain conditions guarantees exponential convergence of the observer regardless of initial error. Additional results are provided that apply this theorem to chemical reaction network models. Moreover, these results are illustrated via examples of mass action form of chemical reaction networks where a subset of the species concentrations are observed. Numerical results are provided to show the efficacy of our proposed observer. Numerical results are also shown for the case of noisy observations and our observer is compared favorably with the particle filter when the observation noise is small.
