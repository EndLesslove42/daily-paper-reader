## 摘要
我们提出了基于随机微分方程（SDEs）的数据同化问题的动态低秩（DLR）型滤波器。具体来说，首先我们推导了一种DLRA滤波器，用于联合最小化均值和协方差误差，并提出了一种有效包含相关正交方向的策略。这最后一种方法允许主子空间也根据观测算子进行演化。这些程序在处理线性漂移时自然扩展到卡尔曼-布西型滤波器，也扩展到集成方法，因此也适用于由非线性漂移和可能的非高斯分布描述的问题。此外，我们进一步提出了一种初步的粒子型DLRA滤波器，在非线性环境中显示出潜力。数值模拟显示了这些程序在相关应用中的有效性，为这些滤波方向的进一步研究开辟了道路。

## Abstract
We propose dynamical low-rank (DLR) type filters for data-assimilation problems based on stochastic differential equations (SDEs). In detail, first we derive a DLRA filter for minimizing jointly the mean and covariance error, as well as a strategy to efficiently include the relevant orthogonal directions. This last approach allows the main subspace to evolve also according to the observation operator. Those procedures naturally extend to a Kalman-Bucy type filter when dealing with linear drift, and to ensemble methods, too, resulting also suitable for problems described by nonlinear drift and possible non-Gaussian distribution. Moreover, we further propose a preliminary particle-type DLRA filter that shows potentiality in nonlinear settings. Numerical simulations show the efficacy of these procedures in relevant applications, opening up to further studies in these filtering directions.
